How it works

Six steps. A person signs off at every gate.

  1. 01 · Teacher

    Upload material

    A PDF, notes or a link, split into topics and course outcomes.

  2. 02 · AI

    Generate a draft

    MCQ, short and essay questions across Bloom's levels. The teacher edits it.

  3. 03 · HOD

    Approve the paper

    Policy checks for outcome coverage and repeats. Approve or return with comments.

  4. 04 · Student

    Attempt the exam

    A timed exam with auto-save, from the class page.

  5. 05 · Teacher

    Grade and review

    AI scores written answers and flags possible AI writing. Only the teacher changes grades.

  6. 06 · Student

    Get feedback

    A topic-by-topic breakdown and a study plan.

Four roles

Everyone sees only their part.

  • TeacherCreates papers, grades and releases feedback.
  • Head of departmentApproves papers and watches quality.
  • StudentTakes timed exams and learns from the results.
  • AdminSets up users, departments and rules.
